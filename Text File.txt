## Wichtige methodische Grenze

In allen 16 Paaren unterscheiden sich die konkreten aktiven Request-Indizes zwischen `no_burst` und `prefill_burst`.

Das ist kein technischer Fehler: Die Kohorte wird dynamisch aus dem tatsächlichen Triggerzustand gebildet. Es bedeutet aber:

> Die Effektzahlen vergleichen die Mediane zweier jeweils gültiger aktiver Kohorten. Sie sind keine Request-für-Request-Matches derselben acht Requests.

Deshalb sollte das Paper nicht von „paired requests“ oder einem individuellen kausalen Request-Effekt sprechen. Zulässig sind „paired episodes“ beziehungsweise „within-block comparison of active-cohort medians“.

## Paper-sichere Kernaussage

> Across four repeats per cell, the client-visible burst-output regime was perfectly associated with the configured sequence capacity: all `max_num_seqs=4` blocks were classified as C, whereas all `max_num_seqs=8` blocks were classified as B, independently of CPU offloading. Offloading did not change the overlap class but increased the interval between completion of the initially active cohort and first burst output by factors of approximately 55–70. Active-cohort TPOT medians remained within 0.085% of the corresponding no-burst condition.

Danach die Scope-Grenze:

> These classes describe client-visible output timing only. They do not reveal internal admission or prefill-start times. Episode comparisons use dynamically identified active cohorts and are not request-identity-matched effects.
