                                        language models have expanded the range of applications they
                                        support, from short interactive exchanges to long, compute-
                                        heavy requests. LLM serving platforms today define response-
                                        latency service-level objectives, even though requests within the
                                        same service can differ by orders of magnitude in input length,
                                        generation length, execution cost, and the availability of reusable
                                        KV-cache state. As a result, requests governed by the same
                                        service level objective have different urgency: after accounting
